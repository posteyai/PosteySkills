# Per-Platform Caption Archetypes

Part of the `postey` skill. Loaded on demand; see SKILL.md "Content Flows".

## 2. Per-Platform Caption Archetypes

### X (Twitter)

- **Archetype:** long-form narrative post in an authoritative, informed-commentator voice. Full flowing paragraphs. No bullet points or numbered lists in organic posts (those belong in threads).
- **Hook:** first line under 10 words, ideally with a specific number or a stand-taking claim. Never open with a time anchor ("Yesterday...", "On Friday...").
- **Body:** conclusions first, evidence second. Real names, real dollar figures, real timelines. Every paragraph must teach something new.
- **Close:** land on a non-obvious insight the reader will quote, or a "future-moment bookmark" close: *"Bookmark this. You'll want it the next time [specific future moment the reader will hit]."* Never generic engagement bait ("What do you think?").
- **Hashtags:** none. **Emojis:** none in organic posts.
- **Length:** know the account's tier. Standard accounts cap at 280 characters; Premium accounts allow long-form (up to 25K). Don't pad; cut anything that doesn't earn its place.
- **Video-forward variant:** when the video is the content, write 3 punchy lines max (scene-setting, one hard stat, short close). No CTA, no hashtags.
- **External links:** never in the main post (see Section 4). Put the link in the first reply.
- **Quote posts:** append the original post URL at the end of the text so it renders as an embed.

### LinkedIn

- **Archetype:** operator how-to. Professional but punchy; "here's a workflow that works," less oracular than X.
- **Structure:** surprising stat or claim (1 sentence) → one-line setup ("Here's why it matters if you actually do X") → 3-4 numbered shifts, 1-2 sentences each → stakes paragraph ("Most teams treat X like Y. The ones who treat it like Z will pull ahead.") → soft CTA ("Comment 'WORD' if you want the guide").
- **Hashtags:** 3-4 lightweight ones at the end. Heavy hashtag blocks hurt reach.
- **Length:** 3,000 char limit; sweet spot 1,000-1,500.

### Instagram (Reels)

- **Archetype:** native creator voice with a comment-mechanic hook: "Comment 'WORD' and I'll send you the guide." If the video has a spoken hook, that spoken line becomes the first line of the caption.
- **Structure:** comment-mechanic hook → one-line setup ("Most people don't realize you can...") → 3-step numbered body → close ("That's it. [Outcome]." + "Follow for more [topic]") → hashtag block.
- **Hashtags:** 8-10, mixing broad and specific.
- **Length:** 2,200 char limit (rarely a constraint). Reels have a hard 90-second video maximum; vertical 9:16 performs best.

### TikTok

- **Archetype:** short native voice. One-line hook, one-line value prop, optional comment CTA, hashtag block.
- **Hashtags:** heavy; #fyp plus 5-7 topic tags.
- **Length:** sweet spot under 200 characters total including hashtags.

### YouTube

- **Regular videos:** title ≤100 chars, pattern "[Verb] [Subject] [Qualifier] ([Hook in parens])". Description mirrors the Instagram caption. 8-15 comma-separated tags mixing broad and specific. Pick the category to match the content.
- **Shorts (≤60s):** punchier title ≤60 chars, no parentheticals, lead with the most surprising fact. Description under 500 chars.
- Flag it when a video's length changes its placement (over 60s means it publishes as a regular video, not a Short).

### Threads

- **Archetype:** the long-form idea compressed into a single post. Casual, slightly more energetic than X.
- **Structure:** compressed hook → one-line core insight → compressed steps in 1-2 sentences (not numbered) → one-line why-it-matters → "Save this." close (never "before it disappears"; that reads as small-creator bait).
- **Hashtags:** 0-2. **Length:** 500 hard cap; aim 400-450. **Media:** one video max per post.

### Bluesky

- **Archetype:** the tightest version of the message. Plain prose: hook sentence, core insight sentence, one-sentence compression of the steps, optional "Save this."
- **Hashtags:** typically none; the culture is hashtag-light.
- **Length:** 300 hard cap; aim ~250. **Media:** video ≤15MB and ≤60s; prepare a dedicated small encode.

### CTA mapping across platforms

The intent of a CTA carries; the mechanic adapts:

| Platform | CTA mechanic |
|---|---|
| Instagram | "Comment 'WORD' and I'll send you the guide" (literal) |
| YouTube | Same as Instagram, in the description |
| LinkedIn | "Comment 'WORD' if you want the full guide." |
| Threads | "Save this." (comment-DM mechanics die here) |
| X | Future-moment bookmark close (comment-DM mechanics die here) |
| TikTok | "Comment 'WORD' for the guide" or skip |
| Bluesky | "Save this." or omit |

Never port the Instagram comment-DM mechanic verbatim onto X; it kills the long-form voice.
