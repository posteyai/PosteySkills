# Thread, Reel, Carousel & Trend Formats

Part of the `postey` skill. Loaded on demand; see SKILL.md "Content Flows".

## 5. Thread Formats (6-15 posts)

Classify before writing. If the content fits none of these, it's probably a single post, not a thread.

| # | Format | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Launch Breakdown** | Major product drop, model release, keynote, roadmap |
| 2 | **Challenger Upset** | An underdog product genuinely beats established incumbents (use sparingly, 1-2x/month) |
| 3 | **Builder Profile** | A founder or builder whose story is genuinely remarkable |
| 4 | **Video Gallery** | 6-15 striking clips on one shocking topic |
| 5 | **Resource Roundup** | 5-10 free tools/resources that solve an expensive problem |
| 6 | **Learning Roadmap** | Step-by-step how-to or free learning path |

**Audience filter:** before writing, ask "would a senior operator or investor find this insightful, or roll their eyes?" Punchy is fine; hype and fan energy get rewritten.

**Hook patterns (fill in the blanks):**
- Launch Breakdown: `BREAKING: [Company] just [bold transformation verb]. / [What launched, one plain sentence.] / [Stakes escalation.] / Here's the full breakdown:`: or the trend variant: `[CATEGORY] is taking over in [YEAR]. [Company] just announced [Product]. Here are [N] wild examples:`
- Challenger Upset: `[Challenger] just dropped [product] that beats [Leader 1], [Leader 2], and [Leader 3]. / [Two specific capabilities + one number.] / Here's everything:`: body tweet 1 introduces the product with a sourced, verifiable result (cite where it comes from; never invent a first-person test you did not run); tweet 2 contrasts the incumbent on the same sourced task.
- Builder Profile: `This is [Name], [Title] at [Company]. / [N] years ago, [dramatic surprising action]. / The journey: 👇`: body is chronological chapters: origin, first inflection, the bold bet (with a number), the setback, the breakthrough (with a metric), where they are now, one-sentence lesson without moralizing.
- Video Gallery: authentic disbelief opener, lowercase and raw, no "BREAKING:". One clip per post, one sentence of context max; the clip is the post.
- Resource Roundup: `Everyone pays $[Y] for [Z], but [problem]. / I found [N] FREE [things] that [specific outcome].`: one resource per post: name, URL, what it does, how to use it, result.
- Learning Roadmap: `[N] things I learned [doing X] that nobody teaches you:`: each post is one actionable, copy-pasteable step.

**Body building blocks:** numbered feature (name + what it does + how to use + proof); numbered announcement with arrow sub-points (→); numbered demo with the exact prompt used. Around post 10, include a proof beat: a benchmark, a named quote, or a counter-take. Close with a follow CTA + bookmark line (curation formats only; keep it out of authoritative long-form voice).

**Universal thread voice rules:** first 3 words must grab; line break after every sentence; verbs over adjectives ("DEFEATED" not "amazing"); specific numbers only; every claim followed by immediate proof; short sentences; max 1-2 emojis per post; no buzzwords, em-dashes, or hashtags; no moralizing close; media on nearly every post (write `[ATTACH: description]` placeholders and flag missing media).

**One documented framing mechanic, use judiciously:** when a company isn't widely known, category- or region-level framing in the hook ("[Country]'s robots just...") often outperforms the company name; name the specific company later in the thread for credibility. Keep the company name when the company itself is the story.



## 6. Reel and Carousel Formats (Instagram)

**Core principle: optimize for Sends** (people DMing the post to a friend), the highest-signal engagement, ahead of views or likes. Saves are a close second. Every format below is engineered to be worth sending.

### Reel types

**1. Timely News Reel**: industry news from the last 24 hours only. "Just announced" must mean today or yesterday; verify recency before writing, or reword. Script: `[0-3s] hook (visual + one-line shock) → [3-15s] what it is → [15-40s] why it matters → [40-55s] personal take or prediction → [55-60s] CTA ("Send this to someone who needs to see it")`.

**2. Brand Storytelling Reel**: for product content, tell stories about the problem, never demo features. The audience should barely notice it's a product post. Frameworks: Before → After → Result (with numbers); The Moment I Realized (the frustration that led to the discovery); Team Pain Point (conflict → fix); The Number (funnel numbers → lesson). Script: hook (relatable pain) → the problem, vividly → the solution shown on screen → the result with a specific number → soft CTA. Cap product promos at roughly 1 in 5 posts.

**3. Top-10 Curation Reel**: the proven follower-growth format. Curated lists (tools, repos, resources) people save and send. Script counts down 10→1 with fast cuts, demos for the strong picks, and a "Save this. Send it to someone who needs it." close. Caption lists all 10 with one-line descriptions plus "Which one are you trying first?". Not too niche: broad enough that a large audience can use the list.

**4. Hot Take Reel**: a contrarian opinion stated bluntly to drive comment debates. Script: the take → 2-3 supporting facts → why the consensus is wrong → prediction → "Agree or disagree? Drop your take." Raw talking-head delivery beats polished editing for opinion content.

### Carousel types

**1. Top-10 List**: cover slide with the promise, one item per slide counting down, CTA slide. Design: 4:5 portrait (1080x1350), dark background, bold type; the ranking number must be one of the largest elements on the slide (it IS the curiosity hook); every slide carries a subtle swipe cue at bottom right.

**2. Step-by-Step Tutorial**: cover ("How to [result] in [N] steps") → one step per slide with a screenshot and instruction → result slide → CTA. Caption repeats the steps in text with "Save this for later."

**3. Comparison**: "[A] vs [B], honest review." Overview slide, 3 feature-round slides, pricing slide, clear-verdict slide, CTA. Honesty is the differentiator: name real cons for both sides, pick a winner, say why.

### IG caption rules

Hook line under 10 words (counter-intuitive claim, stat, or question) → 2-4 sentences of narrative with specific numbers → a close that drives sends or saves. First person. No em-dashes, no buzzwords. Specific > vague. Talk to a smart friend, not a conference room.



## 7. Trend-Content Formats (the 5 pillars)

A repeatable daily/weekly mix for accounts covering a fast-moving industry. Every post carries: the agent tag + a category tag + 2 topic tags.

| # | Pillar | Format | Rule of thumb per run |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Video Clips** | 1-3 min clips mined from long-form interviews, podcasts, talks | 2-3 |
| 2 | **Breaking News** | Text-based, from official blogs, press releases, company posts | Only if it broke in the last hour |
| 3 | **Industry Drops** | Text breakdowns of what major players shipped or announced recently | 1-2 |
| 4 | **Innovation Showcase** | Real-world engineering/infrastructure breakthroughs; video is mandatory | 1 |
| 5 | **Leader Stories** | Timeless video clips of well-known founders/leaders sharing hard-won lessons | 2-3 |

**Pillar rules:**
- **Video Clips:** mine transcripts of long-form sources for standout segments. Ranking criteria: counter-intuitive statements, emotional vulnerability, bold falsifiable predictions, existential stakes, raw honesty about failure, life-changing frameworks, unfiltered opinions. Quality gates: 60-180 seconds; understandable standalone; clear hook in the first 5 seconds; passionate delivery beats scripted; specific numbers beat vague claims.
- **Breaking News:** "BREAKING" is reserved for events from the last ~60 minutes. Same-day-but-older news gets covered as an Industry Drop instead; never both for one story. If nothing qualifies, skip the pillar entirely; do not force it.
- **Industry Drops:** maintain a tracking list of the major players in your niche; summarize what shipped with specifics and a take on what it changes.
- **Innovation Showcase:** the video is the hook and the caption explains why it matters. No video available = pick a different innovation; never run this pillar as text.
- **Leader Stories:** not news, not product demos; timeless lessons through real stories. Before drafting, search the account's own post history for the key phrases; skip any angle already covered.

**Why video-heavy:** video content substantially outperforms text-only on X (see Section 4), which is why the two video pillars anchor the mix. Quality over quantity: don't burn 20 minutes hunting a third clip when the other pillars are ready.
