## 3. Hook Formulas

### The pain + number 3-line formula

For conversion-oriented posts:

```
Line 1: The pain or fact, under 10 words, with a specific number
Line 2: The reward, what the reader GETS (a large value gap)
Line 3: The action, one clear CTA
```

Rules:
- Line 1 is a pain or fact, never a feature. "Agencies charge $3,000/month" beats "Our tool schedules posts."
- Line 2 is what the reader gets, not what the product does.
- Specific numbers beat vague claims: "saves 12 hours/week" stops the scroll; "saves time" is ignored.
- Number hooks outperform question hooks for conversion, because a number implies a repeatable system and makes the reader ask "how?"
- No filler trust-begging ("no credit card," "no catch," "no strings attached").

### The 10-variations rule

Never stop at one hook. Write 10 distinctly different hooks for the same post. The first 3 are the obvious ones; the best is usually #7-#10. When presenting direction options to a user, offer 5-10 distinct angles.

### The human test

Read the draft out loud. If it sounds like a marketing email, rewrite it. If it sounds like a friend making an offer or telling you something interesting, keep it.

### The cold-read test

The first 2-3 lines of every caption (and every post in a thread) must stand alone; readers arrive mid-scroll with zero context. Common failures: the hook depends on a setup sentence further down; the hook is generic ("Here are 5 things..."); the hook is a question the reader doesn't care about (reframe it as a claim).

### The AI-tells scrub (run on every draft)

- **No em-dashes.** Replace with a comma, a period + new sentence, or a colon.
- **No stacked period-rhythm phrases.** "Zero costs. Zero limits. Fully private." reads as AI cadence; merge or comma-join. One short standalone sentence is fine; three in a row is the tell.
- **Banned buzzwords:** unlock, unleash, leverage (verb), supercharge, 10x/100x (as modifier), game-changer, revolutionize, disrupt, paradigm shift, next-level, crushing it, move the needle. Replace with concrete language about what actually happens.
- **No branded mission-statement closers** ("The future of X is Y."). Cut entirely.
- **No dramatic filler** ("Nobody is talking about this," "This blew my mind"). State the insight directly.
